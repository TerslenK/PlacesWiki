Places - If you want to enter the Places - find an abandoned house in a plains or a forest biome
└── No turning back - It's eternity in here.
    ├── My tongue tastes yellow - Find and roast some Backshrooms
    ├── The endless yellow - Explore every 0 biome
    ├── Everywhere At The End Of Time - Visit every Places dimension
    ├── I'm Mold! - Find some Mold Spores. Mold Spores can be farmed and turned into slime
    ├── Interior Designer - Collect every Wallpaper Pattern
    ├── Iron Stomach - Eat every flavor of Candy Bars
    ├── Home is a Place - Find an odd looking Cherry Door in 0 and enter m
    ├── C0d3Br34k3r - Obtain the Operator Floppy
    ├── Recycling - Obtain Scrapwood from either punching skirting or recycling wooden blocks
    ├── Rest Spot - Craft a Rest Stop Sign to be able to set your spawn within the Places
    ├── Iron Stomach (Sodas) - Drink every flavor of Soda
    ├── Taste the Rainbow - Eat every color of crayon
    ├── Redshift - Find a portal in 0 and Enter R
    ├── Disconnect - Explore far enough in level 0 and get to the Ridge
    │   └── ...The End is never The End is... - Explore the farther regions of 0 or a and enter ->|
    │       ├── Can I see the logs? - Obtain Item Error from slaying an Errot mob.
    │       └── Structural Integrity - Run the Operator Floppy, find a newly opened portal and enter the Structure Bridge
    │           ├── Bugfixing - Obtain Debug Item
    │           │   ├── I can fix her - Obtain the Debugger
    │           │   ├── Personal Qualia - Enter your Pocket Grid
    │           │   ├── This really brings you back - Obtain an Unbound Key. Unbound Keys can be used for fast travel
    │           │   └── So many doors... - Obtain an Unbound Structure Key
    │           │       └── Keygen - Obtain every Structure Key
    │           │           └── advancements.exit_key.title - advancements.exit_key.descr
    │           └── Form given Form - Obtain Structure Shards

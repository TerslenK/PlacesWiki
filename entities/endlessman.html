// Description
Endlessmen are tall black humanoid creatures rooted to the ground with bright glowing magenta eyes.
// Behavior
Endlessmen have never been observed moving or walking and seem to always know where you are and follow you with their eyes. As soon as a person is in an Endlessman's line of sight, their vision darkens and the lights around begin to rapidly flicker. If someone were to come up too close to an Endlessman — the Endlessman seems to vanish or teleport away and damages the one who approached them.
// Biology
Endlessmen have been theorized to not actually be a unique species but rather regular Endermen that have been afflicted by some kind of infection or ailment. They appear slouched and deformed, and the root-like growths at the ends of some of its limbs appear to be rooting the creature to the ground, making it unable to move by normal means.
// Do's and Don'ts

Do's

  • Hide behind a wall or any sort of cover if you get spotted by an Endlessman.
  • Turn back if you hear sounds loosely resembling speech.

Don'ts

  • Try to approach or physically harm them.

guest@places:~/entities/wallpeeper$ cat description.txt
Wallpeepers are 3 block tall yellow 4 legged entities reminiscent of creepers.
guest@places:~/entities/wallpeeper$ cat behavior.txt
Unlike Overworld Creepers, instead of wandering around in search for their victim, Wallpeepers stay dormant disguising themselves as a wall or a pillar until something wanders up too close. Other than their ability to morph into blocks they act mostly like regular Creepers.
guest@places:~/entities/wallpeeper$ cat biology.txt
Wallpeeper's "skin" is very similar in tone to the Yellow Wallpaper that their habitat is comprised of and even shares a similar (but not identical) pattern. The material their bodies are made of are also very similar to a Wallpaper block which makes them a bit bulkier than your average Creeper. They're also really hard to get around as essentially these creatures are walls with legs. Even though the creatures have 4 legs they're not particularly fast. On death drop gunpowder.
guest@places:~/entities/wallpeeper$ cat guidelines.txt

Do's

  • Look out for wallpaper with irregular patterns or faces when exploring new rooms.
  • If you spotted a dormant Wallpeeper, break the blocks while keeping your distance.

Don'ts

  • If a Wallpeeper has spotted you it is advised to run away to not risk an explosion.
  • Do not approach any suspicious looking pillars in places where there shouldn't be any.
